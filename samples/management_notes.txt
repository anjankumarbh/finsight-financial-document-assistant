ABC Manufacturing - management review notes

Reporting context
- The figures cover FY2022 to FY2025 for the consolidated manufacturing group.
- Values are illustrative sample data. Currency and reporting units are not specified.

FY2025 observations
- Revenue increased to 1,285, continuing the growth seen in the prior years.
- Net profit declined to 68 even though revenue increased.
- Operating cash flow declined to 42 while inventory and trade receivables increased.
- Capital expenditure and total debt increased during the expansion period.

Management review points
- Review the expense breakdown for raw material, logistics, energy, and employee costs.
- Review inventory ageing, customer collection days, and supplier payment terms.
- Compare the return from capital expenditure with the additional debt funding.
- Do not draw a final conclusion without the detailed expense and working-capital schedules.
